      Dangerous conditions can exist on the Fairgrounds. Due to exposure to the weather, areas may become slippery or soft. Due to the nature of the goods displayed, heavy and sometimes dangerous equipment and moving vehicles are frequently present. Carlisle Events cannot be responsible for the conduct of vendors and patrons entering these premises. Those who enter the Fairgrounds do so at their own risk.
