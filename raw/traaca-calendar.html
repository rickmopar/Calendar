Come to a monthly Dinner Meeting!

Visitors are always welcome at our Dinner Meetings usually starting at 6:00 p.m. on the third Thursday each month.  Dinner reservations need to be made in advance to ensure adequate seating.   Each meeting includes a special presentation or guest speaker, and we always have a great time socializing. Note that our Dinner Meetings are currently being held at the Holiday Inn Convention Center (5655 Greenwich Road, Virginia Beach VA), and we sometimes substitute special activities, so check the calendar below for actual Dinner Meeting dates and locations.

For more information on meetings and information on making reservations, contact the webmaster by clicking here.
